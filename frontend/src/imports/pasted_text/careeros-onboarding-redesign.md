GLOBAL DESIGN RULES
==================================================

Visual Style:
- premium
- minimal
- modern AI SaaS
- light-first
- soft off-white background
- clean white surfaces
- blue, cyan, violet accents
- restrained gradients
- generous whitespace
- 16–20px rounded cards
- subtle shadows
- outlined icons
- polished but calm

Dark Mode:
Use neutral charcoal surfaces, not black.

Avoid:
- excessive neon
- glowing borders everywhere
- random gradient blobs
- giant glass cards
- too many decorative illustrations
- large blocks of text
- constant animation
- cyberpunk styling

==================================================
ANIMATION RULES
==================================================

Use subtle motion only.

Hero:
Soft fade-up.

Feature Cards:
Small hover lift.

CareerOS Ecosystem:
Animated connecting lines.

Role Cards:
Subtle border/accent change on hover.

Pricing:
Smooth tab transitions.

Theme Switch:
150–250ms transition.

Do not animate everything simultaneously.

==================================================
RESPONSIVE RULES
==================================================

Desktop:
- 4 role cards in one row
- pricing side-by-side
- generous whitespace

Tablet:
- role cards in 2x2 grid
- pricing stacked or tabbed

Mobile:
- single-column role cards
- pricing stacked
- horizontal feature carousel only if necessary
- sticky primary CTA allowed
